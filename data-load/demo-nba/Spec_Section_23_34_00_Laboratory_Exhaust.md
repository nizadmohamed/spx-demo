# SECTION 23 34 00: LABORATORY EXHAUST FANS
Project: Halston University Biosciences Building, Raleigh NC
Issued: Design Development set, Addendum 2
Engineer of record: Momentum Design

## PART 1: GENERAL

### 1.1 SUMMARY
A. Furnish and install induced-draft high-plume laboratory exhaust fans serving the chemistry and biosafety wings.

### 1.2 SUBMITTALS
A. Submit fan curves, sound data, and a wind-tunnel or CFD exhaust dispersion study stamped by a qualified engineer.
B. The dispersion study is required for bid. Bidders without a study at bid time will be considered non-responsive.

### 1.3 SUBSTITUTIONS
A. Basis of design: the induced-draft high-plume fan by Rival Fan Systems, series HP-4.
B. Requests for substitution (or equal) shall be submitted to the engineer no later than 10 days before the bid date of June 15, 2027, which is June 5, 2027. Requests received later will not be reviewed.
C. A substitution request must include a dispersion study that shows equal or better plume rise at the stated exit velocity.

## PART 2: PRODUCTS

### 2.1 FANS
A. Quantity: 6 fans, N+1 redundancy per wing.
B. Minimum exit velocity: 3,000 feet per minute at design flow.
C. Wind-tunnel verified plume height at 2.5 times building height or better.
D. Fan wheel and housing: spark-resistant construction, corrosion-resistant coating suitable for fume hood exhaust.

### 2.3 ACCEPTABLE MANUFACTURERS
A. Rival Fan Systems (basis of design).
B. Other manufacturers are acceptable only through the substitution process in 1.3.

### 2.5 ENERGY RECOVERY
A. The owner has asked that the lab exhaust design allow for a future heat-recovery loop. Provide the stack with capped taps for a later retrofit.

## PART 3: EXECUTION

### 3.1 COMMISSIONING
A. Commissioning agent witnesses start-up of each fan. Provide a 2-year parts and labor warranty.

### 3.2 SCHEDULE
A. Delivery of fans is required 20 weeks after approved submittals. Long-lead fans must be released with the mechanical early-release package.
